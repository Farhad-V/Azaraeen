## Technical debt and refactoring

## Purpose

Record maintainability problems that affect change cost, reliability or security and distinguish them from new features and defects.

## Topics and outputs

For each item capture evidence, impact, proposed improvement, affected requirements/modules and verification needed to preserve behavior. Prioritize against actual project needs.

## Boundaries

No refactoring is scheduled or implementation change authorized merely by listing a topic.

Detailed artifacts and evidence are pending unless explicitly stated above. This outline does not establish a completed assessment or implementation.
