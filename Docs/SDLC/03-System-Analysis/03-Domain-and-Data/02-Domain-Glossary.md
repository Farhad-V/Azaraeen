## Domain glossary

## Purpose

Establish shared terms used in requirements, analysis, design and implementation.

## Topics and outputs

Application: one job application associated with a company and position. Access: a credential set with validity and history. Visit: an authenticated session counted according to the agreed counting rule. Submitted resume: the archived version sent for a specific application. Main resume: the owner-designated version for direct requesters. Publication: owner-approved content visible according to its public/private setting.

## Boundaries

Refine definitions when ambiguity is discovered. Optional contacts do not change company/position attribution or establish verified identity.

Detailed artifacts and evidence are pending unless explicitly stated above. This outline does not establish a completed assessment or implementation.
