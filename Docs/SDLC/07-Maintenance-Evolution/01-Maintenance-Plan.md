# Maintenance Plan

These documents cover operations, support, recovery and changes after release.


## Operations and maintenance

## Purpose and activities

Review errors, correct defects, apply necessary updates and maintain recoverable data.

## Expected outputs

Operating guidance, backup/recovery checks and verified fixes. No support schedule or SLA is assigned.

## Working material

Outline only. The sections above describe intended work; completed artifacts, decisions and verification evidence have not yet been added.


## Monitoring, incidents and recovery

## Work topics

Review operational errors and mail failures, investigate incidents and verify backups can restore required data.

## Expected artifacts

Incident findings, recovery evidence and follow-up actions. Use approved diagnostic data without expanding visitor analytics.

## Current material

Topic outline. Decisions, implementation and verification evidence will be added as the work is performed.


## Post-launch evaluation

## Purpose and activities

Use owner feedback, observed usability issues and agreed basic access counts to evaluate improvements.

## Expected outputs

Findings and prioritized improvements. No page/download tracking or additional visitor analytics are introduced.

## Working material

Outline only. The sections above describe intended work; completed artifacts, decisions and verification evidence have not yet been added.


## Fixes, dependencies and releases

## Work topics

Triage defects and necessary dependency/security updates, evaluate impact and release verified changes.

## Expected artifacts

Change records, regression evidence and links to release procedures. New features return to scope/requirements review; no maintenance schedule or SLA is imposed.

## Current material

Topic outline. Decisions, implementation and verification evidence will be added as the work is performed.


## Technical debt and refactoring

## Purpose

Record maintainability problems that affect change cost, reliability or security and distinguish them from new features and defects.

## Topics and outputs

For each item capture evidence, impact, proposed improvement, affected requirements/modules and verification needed to preserve behavior. Prioritize against actual project needs.

## Boundaries

No refactoring is scheduled or implementation change authorized merely by listing a topic.

Detailed artifacts and evidence are pending unless explicitly stated above. This outline does not establish a completed assessment or implementation.


## Owner documentation and troubleshooting

## Purpose

Maintain current guidance for content publication, translations, access creation/renewal/revocation, application archives and common operational problems.

## Topics and outputs

Link the release-time owner guide as the initial source and record subsequent instruction updates and troubleshooting findings. Keep credentials and private recipient data out of examples.

## Boundaries

This category owns maintained guidance after release; Deployment owns the release checks and initial delivery. No helpdesk staffing or SLA is assumed.

Detailed artifacts and evidence are pending unless explicitly stated above. This outline does not establish a completed assessment or implementation.
