# Maintenance Plan

After launch, monitor availability, access-request mail outcomes, error rates and backup completion. Verify restoration of both the data store and private files in an isolated environment.

For an access or confidentiality incident, contain the affected scope, revoke relevant grants where appropriate, preserve diagnostic records and verify recovery. Keep credentials and confidential content out of routine logs.

Review dependency updates and defects against the regression checks. Samira owns follow-up decisions about archive retention, content and access policy. Record maintenance changes alongside release and verification evidence.
