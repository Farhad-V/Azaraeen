# Product Vision

## Audience and problem

Samira needs a professional portfolio for employers and recruiters that presents her profile clearly, gives approved visitors access to detailed work and documents, and preserves her job-application history.

## Product direction

Azaraeen combines a public professional introduction, owner-controlled protected content and a private application archive. Visitors should understand the profile before entering a password; approved visitors should reach the protected content through one simple entry flow. Samira should retain control over publication and access and retrieve the exact documents submitted for each application.

## Expected value

- Clear professional presentation for employers and recruiters.
- Controlled sharing of detailed work and personal documents.
- Reliable application history and submitted-version retrieval.
- English/German presentation with owner-reviewed translation.
