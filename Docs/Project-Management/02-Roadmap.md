# Roadmap

## Agreed direction

Deliver the agreed MVP, then consider improvements outside its scope. This is an outcome-based direction, not a dated schedule or a promise of a fixed sprint cadence.

## MVP outcome

A usable public/protected bilingual portfolio, owner administration, access lifecycle and email request flow, plus the private searchable application archive with correct submitted resume versions. The authoritative scope is linked from [MVP Scope](03-MVP-Scope.md).

## Subsequent candidates

Multiple templates, advanced page-building and automatic application-correspondence import remain deferred candidates, not approved releases. Detailed visitor page/download tracking is outside MVP and is not introduced by this roadmap.
