# Decisions and Open Questions

Samira decides product scope, content publication and access policy; Farhad collaborates on project delivery. This register indexes decisions and questions without duplicating their detailed source documents.

| Topic | Current position | Primary source |
|---|---|---|
| Product direction | Personal portfolio and private application management | [Product Vision](01-Product-Vision.md) |
| MVP scope | Agreed inclusions/exclusions | [Scope](03-MVP-Scope.md) |

## Recording changes

For a new decision or question, record its identifier, context, options, decision/status, rationale, affected sources and any superseded decision. Leave unknowns explicit. Detailed architecture decisions remain in [Architecture Decisions](../SDLC/04-System-Design/01-Architecture/03-Architecture-Decisions.md); link them here when product impact warrants it.
